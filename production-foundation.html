SECURITY BOUNDARY

What moves off GitHub Pages before accepting real customer data.

Move private

Assessment answers • customer identity • payment status • signal weights • routing rules • matching logic • full proprietary possibility metadata • generated Blueprints.

Keep public

Marketing copy • sample Blueprint • educational Score logic if desired • FAQs • product explanation • public legal notices.

IMPLEMENTATION GATES

Do not call the system production-ready until these gates pass.

A

Legal & Consent

Final privacy policy, terms, refund/cancellation policy, consent records, data retention and deletion/export process.

B

Identity & Access

Verified email, secure authentication, authorization checks, passwordless or modern credential flow, session security.

C

Payments

Hosted checkout, signed webhook verification, idempotency, payment state reconciliation and no raw card handling.

D

Data Protection

Encryption in transit/at rest, secrets management, least privilege, backups, logging and incident procedures.

E

Intelligence QA

Versioned rules, reason-code auditability, test personas, human pilots, no unsupported psychological or market claims.

F

Delivery

Server-generated Blueprint, secure storage, private download links, version history and customer support path.

FOUNDING CLIENT RELEASE

A deliberately small first commercial cohort.

The first release should prioritize reliability, learning and personal service over automation. Keep the Founding Client price at $595 as already planned, while the regular price remains planned at $995. The system should make clear that payment is not live until a hosted provider is connected.
