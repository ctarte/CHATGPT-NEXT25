NEXT25™ PHASE 10 — MATCHING ENGINE 2.0 + INTELLIGENT BLUEPRINT

PURPOSE
Move from simple weighted matching to an explainable selection architecture that distinguishes preferences, aversions and true constraints.

MATCHING LAYERS
1. Positive signals — desired attributes and priorities.
2. Negative signals / friction — activities or structural features the user wants less of.
3. Hard constraints — conditions stated as true must-haves. Constraint failure receives a large penalty and normally removes the possibility from the viable pool.
4. Tension detection — competing desires are surfaced, not averaged away.
5. Diversity rules — avoid returning nine near-duplicates from one possibility family.
6. Discovery tiers — 3 Strong Fits, 3 Adjacent Possibilities, 3 Overlooked Possibilities.
7. Explainability — why surfaced, what could make it wrong, experiment before commitment.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS
- A preference is not a constraint.
- A fit score is not a probability.
- A high aggregate score does not erase a failed must-have.
- A tension is not a defect in the user.
- “Overlooked” does not mean “better.”
- Questionnaire fit is not evidence of real-world enjoyment, demand, earnings or feasibility.

PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT
For each surfaced possibility:
Title / family
Why it surfaced
Which answers/signals contributed
Which constraints were checked
Potential friction / poor-fit conditions
Confidence / evidence quality (not probability of success)
30- or 90-day experiment
Evidence sought
Adjacent alternatives

FUTURE ENGINE
Production should ingest the full Deep Discovery assessment automatically rather than ask the user to recreate a short matching profile. It should use normalized signals, separate hard/soft rules, support missing data, record reason codes, version weights, and generate deterministic auditable results before narrative generation.

SECURITY
Current client-side engine and public JSON are prototypes. Production assessment data, weights, reason codes, full library and customer identity should be protected server-side.
