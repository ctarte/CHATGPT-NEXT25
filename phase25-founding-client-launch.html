OPERATIONS

Design the exceptions before customers find them.

Payment exception

Paid but no entitlement? Reconcile the signed provider event—never ask the customer to pay twice.

Research exception

Unsupported income or licensing claim? Block approval until it is sourced, bounded or removed.

Delivery exception

Wrong customer/version? Stop delivery and open a privacy/security incident immediately.

PROFESSIONAL REFERRALS

The CPA / attorney / advisor channel remains reserved.

During the pilot, referral source can be recorded simply and consentfully. The polished “For Professionals” pathway follows once the core paid workflow is stable—without re-cluttering the primary customer journey.
