WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Preference

“I'd rather not sell.”

Must-have

“I do not want a next chapter that depends heavily on selling.”

Tension

“I want ownership and high income, but I also want very low uncertainty and little selling.”

NEXT25 should not erase that tension with a high average score. It should show it.

EXPLAINABILITY STANDARD

“Why this?” should always have an answer.

A recommendation-like black box would be the wrong product. NEXT25 should be able to show the signals, constraints and friction that caused a possibility to appear.
