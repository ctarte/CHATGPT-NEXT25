REJECTION INTELLIGENCE™

Don't reject the title.
Understand the reason.

A customer may reject consulting because of emergencies—not because they dislike problem solving. They may reject teaching because of public visibility—not because they dislike mentoring. NEXT25 separates the occupation from the underlying work design.
