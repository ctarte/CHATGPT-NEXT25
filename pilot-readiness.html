Customer Journey Audit

READY1 • Discover & Evaluate

Commercial homepage, How It Works, Second Life Architect™, About and Sample Blueprint now present a coherent offer.

REFINE2 • Decide & Enroll

The $595 Founding Client offer and enrollment flow exist. Before launch, final refund/cancellation language, service scope and customer support/contact route must be explicit.

BLOCKER3 • Pay & Activate

No live hosted payment or verified entitlement exists. Production must use hosted checkout and server-side confirmation; a browser success redirect cannot prove payment.

BLOCKER4 • Complete Discovery

The assessment is a strong prototype, but it stores responses in the browser. Do not collect paying-customer discovery responses until protected persistence and authorization are implemented—or use an approved high-touch secure intake process for the pilot.

BLOCKER5 • Generate & Deliver Blueprint

Blueprint logic and sample output exist, but secure production generation, human QA and private delivery are not yet connected.

REFINE6 • Activate & Follow Up

90-Day Experiments™ are defined. Pilot operations still need a clear follow-up cadence and a simple way to record experiment evidence and customer feedback.

THE FIVE LAUNCH BLOCKERS

Do not accept the first payment until these are resolved.

  1. Final legal + commercial termsPrivacy, Terms, refund/cancellation, service scope, consent version and customer contact/support information reviewed for launch.
  2. Hosted payment + verificationUse a PCI-compliant hosted checkout; verify payment server-side before granting access. Never collect raw card data in NEXT25 forms.
  3. Protected customer data pathAuthentication or an approved secure high-touch alternative; authorization, protected storage, export/deletion and recovery procedures.
  4. Private Blueprint workflowAssessment → signal/matching run → human quality review → private PDF/document delivery. Keep proprietary production logic out of the public bundle.
  5. Operational support + incident pathNamed support channel, failed-payment handling, lost-access procedure, correction workflow and a simple incident-response plan.

RECOMMENDED FIRST COHORT

High-touch by design.

For the first 5–10 Founding Clients, automate only what improves safety or customer experience. Keep Blueprint quality review, onboarding support and follow-up deliberately human while NEXT25 learns what real customers find useful.

RELEASE DECISION

Current status: NOT YET READY TO ACCEPT PAYMENT.

The commercial proposition is ready enough to pilot. The transaction, protected-data and private-delivery layers are not. Phase 18 therefore freezes major public-site redesign and makes production safety and pilot operations the priority.
