WHY DEEPER QUESTIONS MATTER

“I need a change” can mean ten different things.

I dislike my profession.

A new field may deserve exploration.

I dislike how I practice it.

A different role or business model may be enough.

I dislike my environment.

The employer—not the expertise—may be the problem.

I am bored.

Novelty, challenge or learning may matter more than retirement.

I want control.

Autonomy may be the real objective behind entrepreneurship.

I want meaning.

Contribution may matter more than occupation.
