ABOUT NEXT25™

Traditional planning organizes resources.
NEXT25 explores what they are for.

NEXT25 is a life-intelligence concept built around a simple observation: many capable adults reach a point where the old answer no longer feels like the right one.

THE QUESTION

What do you want the years ahead to actually contain?

Financial planning can help organize money. Career planning can help evaluate work. Estate planning can help prepare what is left behind. Those disciplines matter—but they do not necessarily answer how you want to spend your time, experience, freedom, curiosity and accumulated knowledge.

NEXT25 was created to occupy that space.
