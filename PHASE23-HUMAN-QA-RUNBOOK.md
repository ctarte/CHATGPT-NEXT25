# Human QA Runbook
Reviewer sees the customer's frozen assessment, extracted signals/reason codes, nine candidates, 2–3 briefs, research evidence and assembled Blueprint side-by-side.

BLOCK if: recommendation contradicts a hard constraint without explanation; income claim lacks basis; licensing/regulatory claim is unsupported; assessment evidence cannot trace a major inference; deep brief lacks actionable start; sensitive/clinical inference appears; customer is told a path is guaranteed/perfect; source is stale for a material current claim.

REVISE if: opportunities are too similar; friction is minimized; first steps are generic; market channels are unconnected to buyer; research limitations are unclear; prose overstates confidence.

APPROVE only when the Blueprint is coherent, individualized, evidence-aware, agency-preserving and useful enough to begin testing.
