NEXT25 INTELLIGENCE ENGINE™ — PHASE 7

PIPELINE
Discovery → Signals → Patterns → Constraints → Fit Filters → Possibility Expansion → Experiments → Blueprint

CORE SIGNALS
Energy: solve, teach, create, build, research, connect, lead, serve, explore.
Transition: retirement, burnout, career change, first business, deferred ambition, simplification.
Expertise: reuse, reshape, monetize selectively, teach, transfer, leave behind.
Freedom: autonomy, flexibility, geography, seasonality, schedule control.
Meaning: contribution, mastery, family, creation, service, impact, legacy.
Constraints: time, financial risk, caregiving, property, geography, energy, uncertainty tolerance.
Activation: curious, willing to experiment, part-time pilot, substantial transition.

DESIGN RULES
Skill does not equal energy. Burnout does not automatically mean dislike of a profession. Interest does not equal fit. A deferred dream may represent an underlying value rather than a literal plan. Every surfaced possibility must explain why it appeared, what could make it wrong, a small experiment, and evidence to observe.

BOUNDARIES
Surface possibilities, not prescriptions. Do not claim a perfect career/business, diagnose health or mental conditions, infer financial capacity, or make investment, tax, legal, medical or clinical recommendations.
