NEXT25™ FOUNDING CLIENT PILOT OPERATIONS — v1.0

COHORT
Recommended first cohort: 5–10 customers. Do not advertise an invented scarcity number; this is an internal operating target.

SERVICE MODEL
High-touch pilot. Automate payment/security/data protection where required. Keep onboarding, Blueprint quality review and follow-up human until real usage validates the workflow.

CUSTOMER RECORD
Maintain only the minimum data needed to provide the service. Separate contact/account information from assessment content where practical.

BLUEPRINT QA — HUMAN REVIEW
Before delivery confirm:
- Customer/assessment/version are correct.
- Summary is traceable to supplied answers.
- Preferences are not presented as hard constraints unless the customer made them hard constraints.
- No medical/psychological diagnosis.
- No inference of financial adequacy or investment suitability.
- No prediction of business/career success.
- Strong/Adjacent/Overlooked possibilities are meaningfully different.
- Each possibility includes why it surfaced, what could make it wrong and an experiment.
- Any factual market/licensing/earnings claim is separately researched and sourced or omitted.
- Tone preserves agency: may, suggests, worth exploring, investigate.
- 90-Day Experiments are bounded and realistic.

FOLLOW-UP
Suggested pilot touchpoints:
Onboarding: confirm access and expectations.
After assessment: confirm completion and flag technical issues.
Blueprint delivery: orient customer to how to read it.
~30 days: what was investigated or tested?
~60 days: what evidence is emerging?
~90 days: what would they repeat, stop, modify or explore next?

PILOT LEARNING QUESTIONS
What felt most personally accurate?
What surprised you?
What felt generic?
Which possibility would you never have considered?
Which possibility was clearly wrong, and why?
Was the reason a possibility surfaced understandable?
Did the Blueprint make the next step easier?
What did you wish NEXT25 had asked?
Would you describe the experience as worth the price? Why or why not?
Do not turn pilot feedback into public testimonials without explicit permission and appropriate review.

INCIDENT / SUPPORT MINIMUM
Have a named support channel before launch.
Document who handles: payment failure, refund, lost access, incorrect customer document, deletion/export request, suspected unauthorized access, and incorrect Blueprint.
