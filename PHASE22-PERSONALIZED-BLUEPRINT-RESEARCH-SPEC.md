# NEXT25 Phase 22 — Personalized Blueprint PDF & Research Engine™
Phase 22 proves the customer deliverable before automating it. A complete fictional customer (Michael Hale, 61) receives a next-chapter narrative, Personality & Work-Style Profile™, nine possibilities, three deep Opportunity Briefs™, income context, customer discovery, first-30-day actions, 90-Day Experiments™, Possibility Gap™, next-90-day plan and research appendix.

Research evidence in the demo is current as of 2026-09-26 and includes authoritative BLS career-income context plus SBA market-research guidance and SCORE implementation resources. Employee wage data is explicitly not treated as consulting/business income. Business income examples are scenarios with visible assumptions, not forecasts.

The package adds evidence contracts, research governance, PDF render contracts and PDF quality standards. The actual fictional PDF is included separately with the Phase 22 deliverables.

Production still needs authenticated research orchestration, reviewer UI, server renderer, private storage, immutable version/checksum workflow and end-to-end tests.
