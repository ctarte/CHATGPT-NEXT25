PHASE 12 • BLUEPRINT QUALITY LAB

Different people should not receive the same life with different nouns.

These fictional personas stress-test whether NEXT25 changes its interpretation, question path, matching emphasis and experiments when the underlying person changes.

QUALITY STANDARD

A Blueprint should pass five tests.

01

Recognition

Would this person say, “That sounds like me”?

02

Differentiation

Would a materially different person receive a materially different Blueprint?

03

Explainability

Can we trace an insight or possibility back to actual answers?

04

Expansion

Does at least one possibility broaden the person's field without becoming random?

05

Actionability

Can the person test something before making a major commitment?
