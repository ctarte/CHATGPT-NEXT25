NEXT25™ PHASE 11 — INTEGRATED SECOND LIFE ARCHITECT™ EXPERIENCE

GOAL
Stop making the customer navigate separate prototypes. Create one continuous experience:
DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → EXPAND → TEST → DESIGN.

CORE PRINCIPLE
Answer once. Carry the information forward. Each stage should deepen the interpretation rather than ask the customer to recreate a profile.

PROTOTYPE FLOW
1 DISCOVER — current chapter, what to leave, what to preserve, personal definition of improvement.
2 UNDERSTAND — energy, unwanted competencies, satisfaction, ownership motives/friction, deferred dream, lifestyle conditions, income, uncertainty, identity, expertise.
3 EXPAND — responses automatically generate Strong / Adjacent / Overlooked possibilities. No separate matching questionnaire.
4 TEST — nine candidates receive experiments; user creates shortlist and defines safety/evidence.
5 DESIGN — Personalized NEXT25 Blueprint™ generated from the same profile.

BLUEPRINT SECTIONS
01 Where You Are Now
02 What We Noticed
03 Tensions & Tradeoffs
04 Nine Possibilities Worth Exploring
05 Possibility Gap™
06 90-Day Experiments™
07 25 for 25™
08 Questions Still Worth Answering
09 Your Next 90 Days

PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
- Replace browser-only storage with authenticated encrypted customer data.
- Keep assessment responses separate from public website analytics.
- Move proprietary signal extraction, weights, constraints, reason codes and possibility library server-side.
- Add account save/resume, versioning, consent, deletion/export controls, secure Blueprint generation and PDF/document rendering.
- Full Deep Discovery should ultimately branch intelligently; the Phase 11 prototype uses a curated integrated question set.
- Preserve reason codes so narrative generation is auditable.
- Do not infer clinical state, financial adequacy, or suitability from lifestyle answers.
- Keep outputs framed as possibilities and experiments, not prescriptions.

PRODUCT EXPERIENCE
The customer should not perceive Score, Deep Discovery, Intelligence Engine, Possibility Universe and Matching Engine as separate products. Those are internal capabilities. Second Life Architect™ is the customer-facing experience.
