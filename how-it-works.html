HOW NEXT25 WORKS

Clarity does not always arrive
before you begin.

NEXT25 helps you move from a vague sense that something should change to a thoughtful set of possibilities you can investigate in the real world.

01

DISCOVER

Start with the life you actually have.

Explore the transition, your energy, the parts of your experience you want to preserve, what you want less of, and the conditions the next chapter needs to support.

02

UNDERSTAND

Look beneath the obvious answer.

Wanting a business may really mean wanting autonomy. Wanting retirement may mean wanting relief from a particular environment. A deferred dream may represent creativity, ownership, adventure or a different identity.

03

EXPAND

Widen the field before narrowing it.

NEXT25 explores Strong, Adjacent and Overlooked possibilities across different configurations of work, learning, creation, contribution, experience and legacy.

04

TEST

Turn attractive ideas into evidence.

A possibility that sounds good on paper may feel very different on an ordinary Tuesday. Small experiments help you learn before making a large commitment.

05

DESIGN

Bring the picture together.

Your Personalized NEXT25 Blueprint™ organizes what emerged, possibilities worth exploring, tradeoffs, experiments, 25 for 25™ and a practical next-90-day direction.

“Do not decide the life before you have tested the life.”

NEXT25 does not choose your future. It helps you ask better questions, see a wider field and gather better evidence for your own decisions.
