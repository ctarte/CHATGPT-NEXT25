FOUNDING CLIENT FAQ

Before you enroll.

Why is the Founding Client price $595?

The Founding Client pilot is intended to introduce the Second Life Architect™ experience to an initial group while NEXT25 refines the customer journey and delivery system. The planned standard price is $995, although future pricing may change.

Will NEXT25 make decisions for me?

No. The system is designed to reveal patterns, improve questions and help you explore or test possibilities. Important decisions remain yours.

Is the Blueprint automatically generated?

NEXT25 is being designed as a structured technology-enabled experience. The exact production workflow for the pilot will be finalized before paid enrollment opens. Recommendations will be framed as possibilities rather than professional advice.

What happens to my information?

The current preview does not transmit enrollment information. Before live enrollment, NEXT25 will publish complete privacy, retention, deletion and security practices and identify relevant service providers.

Can I see an example first?

Yes. The website includes a fictional Sample NEXT25 Blueprint™ so you can understand the intended structure, depth and tone before purchasing.
