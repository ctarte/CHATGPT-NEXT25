# NEXT25 Phase 20 — Production Application Foundation™
Adds `production-app/` Next.js/TypeScript scaffold; server-only secrets; Stripe Checkout/webhook scaffolds; Supabase Blueprint/Opportunity Brief RLS migration scaffold; customer dashboard; private PDF delivery contract; income/customer/employer research standard; and continued color/photography track.

Career income uses current sourced wage/outlook data with geography/date/measure/limitations. Business income uses transparent scenarios, separates revenue/costs/profit and never guarantees earnings. Business briefs explain where to find likely customers; career briefs explain where/how to find likely employers.

Approved Blueprint becomes a branded, versioned PDF stored privately and downloadable only by the authorized customer through controlled short-lived access.

This is not yet deployed production infrastructure. Provider accounts, real environment variables, complete migrations/RLS tests, auth implementation, PDF renderer, final legal documents and end-to-end tests remain launch requirements.
