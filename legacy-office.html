LEGACY OFFICE™

What should survive you?

Preserve the meaning conventional estate documents were never designed to capture.

MORE THAN ESTATE DOCUMENTS

Preserve what matters—so the people you love inherit more than assets.

A will can say who receives property. It rarely preserves why you made important decisions, what you learned, the stories behind family traditions or what you hope future generations remember.

My Story

Childhood, turning points, relationships, career, challenges and achievements.

My People

Parents, spouse or partner, children, grandchildren, mentors and friends.

My Wisdom

Lessons, beliefs, mistakes, decisions, principles and practical knowledge.

My Traditions

Holidays, recipes, rituals, sayings, places and celebrations.

My Messages

Intentional letters, recordings and milestone messages for particular people or moments.

My Impact

Giving, service, causes, volunteer history and meaningful contribution.

My Archive

Photographs, documents, recordings, timelines and finished family legacy works.

MY 100 STORIES™

You do not have to write a memoir.

Capture one meaningful story at a time by writing, dictating, audio or video. Over time, individual answers can become a remarkable family record.

Legacy is not only what you leave. It is what you intentionally preserve while you are here.
