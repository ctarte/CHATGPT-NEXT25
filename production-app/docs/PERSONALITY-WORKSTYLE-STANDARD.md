# Personality & Work-Style Profile™ Standard
NEXT25 uses personality-adjacent work-style dimensions to improve opportunity design. It is not a clinical or psychological diagnosis and does not assess intelligence, competence, mental condition or fixed identity.

Dimensions: Social energy; collaboration; structure; novelty; ambiguity tolerance; public visibility; selling/prospecting comfort; leadership preference; creative expression; analytical depth; teaching/mentoring inclination; pace; risk/uncertainty comfort; autonomy.

Each signal retains value, confidence and assessment evidence/reason code. Low-confidence signals are questions to test, not facts.

Personality/work style is one layer alongside interests, energy, experience, skills, values, lifestyle, income objectives, time commitment, constraints, risk, deferred dreams and desired impact. No personality dimension alone selects or rejects an opportunity.

## Friction redesign
Before discarding a promising opportunity, test whether the operating model can be redesigned. Examples: selling friction -> referrals/partners/fewer higher-value clients; lower visibility -> research/writing/private advisory/small groups; high autonomy -> independent/fractional/project structures; high collaboration -> partnership/team/cohort delivery; lower ambiguity comfort -> bounded or established operating models when otherwise appropriate. These are hypotheses, not prescriptions.
