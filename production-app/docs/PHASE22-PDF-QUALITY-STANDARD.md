# Personalized NEXT25 Blueprint™ PDF Quality Standard
The PDF is a premium client deliverable, not a transcript.

Required: branded cover; executive narrative; What We Noticed; Personality & Work-Style Profile™; tensions; nine possibilities; 2–3 deep Opportunity Briefs; income context; customer/employer acquisition; first 30 days; 90-Day Experiments™; Possibility Gap™; next 90 days; research appendix; boundaries.

Visual: readable 11.5pt+ body where practical, generous margins/white space, navy/ivory/gold anchors with restrained sage/slate/clay accents, editorial photography used as chapter rhythm rather than decoration, accessible contrast, page numbers, repeated brand footer.

Production: human approval before render; immutable content/template versions; private storage; checksum; short-lived authorized download; historical versions preserved.
