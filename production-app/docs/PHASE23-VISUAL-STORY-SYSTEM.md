# Phase 23 Visual Story System
Continue navy/ivory/gold as anchors with restrained sage (renewal/learning), slate (intelligence/trust) and clay (creative action).

Photography becomes categorical:
- Reinvention: threshold/horizon/movement
- Career: purposeful contemporary work, not corporate handshake clichés
- Business: real making/serving/teaching/small-enterprise environments
- Experience: exploration and learning, not retirement-ad beach imagery
- Legacy: intergenerational/story/objects/history, not sentimental stock clichés

Production target remains roughly 8–12 strong licensed/original editorial assets across the commercial site. Every image must advance the story, crop responsively, retain readable overlays and include meaningful alt text when informative.
