# Surprise Me™ Exploration
Three customer-controlled breadth modes:
1. Build on What I Know
2. Take Me Somewhere Adjacent
3. Surprise Me

Surprise Me intentionally reduces career-identity anchoring. It should search across interests, deferred dreams, environments, modes of contribution, ownership, making, exploration, learning and legacy while preserving hard constraints.

Quality guardrail: surprising does not mean random. Every surfaced possibility still needs an explainable connection to evidence and a reason it deserves investigation.
