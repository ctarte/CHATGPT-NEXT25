# Founding Client Service Standard
The pilot is intentionally high-touch. Automation should remove avoidable friction, not remove judgment.

Customer receives Second Life Architect™ discovery, Personality & Work-Style Profile™, nine possibilities, 2–3 deep Opportunity Briefs™, material current research, a human-reviewed Personalized NEXT25 Blueprint™ PDF and 30/60/90 experiment follow-up prompts.

Do not promise a fixed delivery time until cohort evidence supports it. Do not promise employment, business success, earnings or a perfect match. NEXT25 informs exploration; the customer chooses.

A report is not complete because it is long. It is complete when the customer can understand why an opportunity surfaced, what it actually entails, what income evidence does and does not show, who to contact, what to do first, what could make it wrong and how to test it without unnecessary commitment.
