# Phase 22 Research Governance
Every time-sensitive implementation claim in an Opportunity Brief must be linked to evidence captured at generation/review time.

Preferred hierarchy: government/regulator/licensing authority -> primary industry/professional body -> credible current industry source -> platform/provider source for platform-specific facts. Community anecdotes may inspire questions but should not establish earnings, licensing or market-demand claims.

Income:
- Career: identify exact occupation mapping, geography, data period and measure. Do not imply a BLS employee wage is consulting income.
- Business: explicit scenario assumptions; separate gross revenue, expenses, profit and owner compensation. Market pricing requires current buyer/source evidence.
- Reject unsupported “average consultant makes...” claims.

Market access: current channels must be verified. Explain why a channel fits the buyer and customer's work style, not merely list websites.

Licensing/regulatory: if the opportunity may be regulated, research the customer's actual jurisdiction before final report. Never infer that no license is required merely because one source is silent.

Research records store as-of date, URL/source identity, supported claim and limitations so human QA can trace the report.
