# Income Context Engine™
Income is decision context, not a promise.
CAREER: store occupation mapping, geography, source/data year, measure, employee/contractor status, limitations and sources. Prefer authoritative labor data when mapping is valid.
BUSINESS: model explicit scenario variables; show gross revenue separately from expenses and illustrative pre-tax owner economics. Assumptions visible. Never label modeled revenue as salary.
Confidence: HIGH direct authoritative mapping; MEDIUM related occupation/multi-source pricing with caveats; LOW sparse/niche market—emphasize interviews and pricing tests. Reviewer rejects income claims missing source/date/basis.
