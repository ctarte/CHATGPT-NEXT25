# Phase 26 Acceptance Tests
## Identity & access
Two fictional customers cannot read each other's assessments, Blueprints or downloads. Customer cannot access Studio. Researcher cannot release. Reviewer cannot bypass payment/entitlement.

## Transaction
Checkout success redirect alone grants nothing. Valid signed payment event grants entitlement exactly once. Duplicate event is harmless. Refund/dispute follows finalized policy.

## Assessment
Save/resume survives new session. Freeze prevents silent mutation. Missing material answer creates clarification, not fabricated inference.

## Intelligence
Hard constraints are honored or explicitly discussed. Nine possibilities are meaningfully different. Every major surfaced reason traces to evidence/reason codes. Low-confidence work-style signals are framed as questions.

## Research
Material current income/market/licensing claims include source identity, as-of date, geography/jurisdiction where relevant, measure and limitation. Business scenarios are labeled assumptions, not forecasts.

## QA/PDF
Approval attaches to exact content version. Any material edit invalidates approval. Renderer uses approved version only. Private PDF ownership/version/checksum are checked before release.

## Operations
Support route, incident path, export/deletion and backup/restore are exercised in staging. Logs contain identifiers/status, not unnecessary assessment narrative.
