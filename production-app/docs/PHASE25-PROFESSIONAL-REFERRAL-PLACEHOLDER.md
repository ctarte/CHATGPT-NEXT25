# Professional Referral Network™ — Phase 25 placement
The CPA / attorney / financial-advisor pathway remains reserved in the launch architecture.

During the first 5–10-customer pilot, record referral source manually only with appropriate consent. Do not launch a complex partner portal yet.

After the core workflow is stable, add “For Professionals” as a secondary public path with:
- transition triggers / who to refer;
- sample Blueprint and client experience;
- professional boundaries;
- referral link/code or warm introduction;
- client-controlled consent;
- no disclosure of assessment/Blueprint to referrer without authorization.

Any compensation, endorsement/testimonial, co-branding or regulated-professional referral arrangement receives separate legal/compliance review before implementation.
