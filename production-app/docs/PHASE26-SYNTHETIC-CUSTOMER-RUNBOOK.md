# First Synthetic Customer Runbook — Elena Brooks
Elena is fictional. No real money, email or personal information is used.

Test the happy path, then deliberately test:
1 duplicate payment webhook;
2 logout/login and assessment resume;
3 missing public-speaking answer;
4 autonomy/collaboration tension;
5 low selling comfort vs business interest;
6 unsupported income claim;
7 research task completion;
8 QA revision after draft;
9 stale approval after content change;
10 wrong-customer/wrong-version PDF release attempt;
11 refund/revoke path;
12 export/deletion request;
13 backup/restore.

Pass standard: each step produces durable evidence and either advances exactly once or blocks safely. A UI success screen is never evidence of a backend transition.
