# Possibility Refinement Loop™ Standard
## Purpose
The first nine possibilities are a high-quality hypothesis field, not a verdict. Customer reaction becomes new evidence.

## Included Founding Client experience
One formal refinement round is included in the proposed $595 pilot service. Before marketing this as a contractual entitlement, incorporate it into final service terms.

## Flow
First Possibility Field -> customer reactions -> adaptive WHY questions -> rejection/attraction evidence -> choose exploration mode -> analyze customer-originated idea(s) -> Second Possibility Field™ -> human QA -> revised Blueprint.

## Reaction handling
Never argue with a customer because a matcher score is high. Ask what they are reacting to. Distinguish title, industry, actual work, buyer, business model, environment, schedule, economics, responsibility and identity.

A rejection can reveal a new hard constraint, but only when the customer clearly states/affirms it. Otherwise preserve it as a preference or hypothesis with confidence.

## Second field quality
Do not produce synonyms for rejected ideas. The second field should demonstrate learning. Explain:
- what the customer rejected and why;
- what remained attractive;
- what new evidence was learned;
- how the search changed;
- why each new/reframed possibility now appears.

Customer can still reject the second field. NEXT25 does not guarantee that a finite list will contain a perfect answer.
