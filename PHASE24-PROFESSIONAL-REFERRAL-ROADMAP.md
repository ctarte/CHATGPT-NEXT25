# NEXT25 Professional Referral Network™ — Roadmap
YES: the public professional/referral path is intentionally coming back.

Why it was removed from the simplified commercial navigation:
The earlier public site had begun to feel crowded. The professional page was moved out of the primary customer journey while the core product, payment, assessment and Blueprint experience were being clarified. That was information-architecture cleanup, not abandonment of the channel.

Recommended timing:
1. Complete and test the paid customer workflow.
2. Run the first small Founding Client cohort and learn where professional referrals fit.
3. Add a dedicated “For Professionals” secondary navigation path and landing experience.
4. Add consent-aware referral tracking only after privacy, compliance and operational rules are finalized.

Audience: CPAs/tax professionals, estate/business attorneys, financial/wealth advisors, succession professionals, and selected transition coaches.

Positioning:
“NEXT25 helps clients explore what they want the next chapter of life to contain. It is designed to complement—not replace—the financial, tax, legal and investment advice they already receive from their professional team.”

Referral experience:
- concise professional explainer;
- ideal client situations / transition triggers;
- sample Blueprint;
- what NEXT25 does / does not do;
- shareable referral link or code;
- warm-introduction option;
- client consent;
- limited status visibility only with consent;
- optional professional resource library later.

Privacy/compliance:
A referral does not authorize NEXT25 to disclose assessment answers, Blueprint content or sensitive client information. Referral compensation, testimonials/endorsements, co-branding and status sharing require separate compliance/legal review based on the referring profession and jurisdiction. Referral source must never influence NEXT25 matching.
