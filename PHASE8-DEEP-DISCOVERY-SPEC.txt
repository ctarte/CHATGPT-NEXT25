NEXT25™ PHASE 8 — DEEP DISCOVERY & BLUEPRINT INTELLIGENCE

OBJECTIVE
Move from surface preferences to explainable interpretation. The system should distinguish motivations, sources of depletion, desired identity, lifestyle requirements, constraints, uncertainty tolerance and willingness to experiment.

DEEP DISCOVERY DOMAINS
1. What Is Changing? — retirement, career change, business, burnout, deferred ambition, intentionality.
2. Energy, Not Just Skill — energizing activities versus competencies the user no longer wants to repeat.
3. Business: Motive vs. Model — ownership, autonomy, income, asset creation, schedule control, idea expression.
4. Deferred Dream — literal idea versus underlying value represented by the dream.
5. The Life It Must Support — schedule, travel, geography, family, income, risk, pace, privacy.
6. Identity & Expertise — preserve, reshape, selectively use or abandon former identity and knowledge.
7. Experiment & Activation — preferred test size, reversibility, evidence and activation barriers.

CONTRADICTION / TENSION DETECTION
Do not call a tension a flaw. Two legitimate preferences may compete.
Examples:
- High autonomy + very low uncertainty tolerance.
- Strong ownership desire + dislike of selling, financial risk and responsibility.
- High income requirement + low commitment / low visibility.
- Desire for geographic freedom + place-bound business model.
- Desire to leave expertise + income expectations dependent on that expertise.
- Desire for a quiet life + highly social/public opportunity.
- Strong creative desire + need for predictable structure.
- Desire to contribute + aversion to people-facing activity.

BLUEPRINT OUTPUT
1. Current Chapter
2. What We Noticed
3. Energy & Motivation Pattern
4. Tensions & Tradeoffs
5. Possibilities Worth Exploring
6. Possibility Gap™
7. 90-Day Experiments™
8. Questions Still Worth Answering

EXPLAINABILITY STANDARD
Every important inference should be traceable to specific answer patterns. Use language such as “may,” “appears,” “suggests,” and “worth exploring.” Avoid diagnostic certainty.

PRODUCTION NOTE
The browser prototype demonstrates logic only. Production assessment answers, proprietary scoring, matching rules, generated Blueprint content and customer identity should move to authenticated server-side infrastructure before commercial use.
