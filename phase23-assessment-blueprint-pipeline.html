BLUEPRINT DEPTH STANDARD™

The top ideas must be deep enough to act on.

Nine possibilities broaden the field. The strongest two or three receive implementation depth: work reality, personality fit, friction, income context, buyers or employers, entry models, gaps, market access, first 30 days, 90-Day Experiment™, poor-fit signals and sources.

THE TEST

Can the customer explain why this surfaced, who might pay or hire, what to do Monday morning, what evidence to gather, and what could make the idea wrong?

EVIDENCE BEFORE CONFIDENCE

If the system doesn't know, it stops.

Missing answers stay missing. Uncertain occupation mappings become research questions. Current income, licensing and market claims require source and date metadata. The pipeline can block rather than manufacture certainty.

HUMAN REVIEW GATE

Automation prepares. A reviewer approves.

The QA rubric checks recognition, traceability, differentiation, work-style fit, depth, income evidence, market access, actionability, agency and research freshness before customer-facing PDF rendering.
