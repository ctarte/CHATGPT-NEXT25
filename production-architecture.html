THE PRODUCTION FLOW

Eight controlled handoffs.

  1. 01

    Enroll

    Review scope, terms, privacy and refund policy.

  2. 02

    Checkout

    Server creates hosted checkout for the fixed product/price.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Signed webhook confirms payment; redirect alone never grants access.

  4. 04

    Activate

    Entitlement becomes active and welcome email is sent.

  5. 05

    Discover

    Authenticated assessment saves protected, versioned responses.

  6. 06

    Research

    Private engine creates possibilities and deep Opportunity Briefs.

  7. 07

    Review

    Human QA checks reasoning, depth, claims and next steps.

  8. 08

    Deliver

    Blueprint is privately stored and securely delivered.

SECURITY BOUNDARY

What leaves the public GitHub bundle.

Private

Customer identity, assessment responses, payment state, signal weights, routing rules, matching logic, proprietary universe metadata, Blueprint drafts, staff notes and final customer documents.

Public

Marketing pages, Sample Blueprint, general methodology, educational Score if desired, FAQs and legal/customer-facing notices.

VISUAL COMPLETION TRACK

Production work should not make the website visually static.

Every release from Phase 19 forward includes visual QA. The final site should use a richer restrained palette and an approved editorial photo library: exploration, purposeful work, creative making, teaching/mentoring, family/legacy and thoughtful travel.

Rule: photography supports the story; it does not become a generic stock-photo gallery.
