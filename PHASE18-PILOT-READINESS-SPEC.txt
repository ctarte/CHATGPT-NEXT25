NEXT25™ PHASE 18 — FOUNDING CLIENT PILOT READINESS™

PURPOSE
Freeze major public-site redesign and determine exactly what separates the current prototype from a safe, coherent first paid Founding Client experience.

AUDIT RESULT
Commercial proposition: sufficiently developed for pilot preparation.
Paid launch: NO-GO today.

FIVE LAUNCH BLOCKERS
1 Final legal/commercial terms and consent for the actual production flow.
2 Hosted payment with server-side verification and entitlement handling.
3 Protected customer assessment/data path.
4 Private Blueprint generation, human QA and private delivery.
5 Operational support, recovery and incident procedures.

PILOT STRATEGY
Start with a small 5–10 customer high-touch cohort. This is an internal operating target, not public scarcity. Automate safety-critical infrastructure; keep onboarding, Blueprint QA and follow-up human.

KEY PRINCIPLE
Do not build for 10,000 customers before learning how to serve the first 10 exceptionally well.
