# Blueprint Depth Standard™
The nine possibilities broaden the field; the top 2–3 Opportunity Briefs™ create practical depth.

A deep brief must answer:
WHY it surfaced; WHAT the work/business actually is; WHAT a normal week could involve; FIT with work style; FRICTION and redesign; WHO pays/hires; ENTRY models; TRANSFERABLE assets; GAPS; MONEY with evidence/assumptions; MARKET access; FIRST 30 DAYS; 90-DAY EXPERIMENT; WHAT COULD MAKE IT WRONG; QUESTIONS before commitment; SOURCES.

Depth is not word count. A brief fails if it sounds impressive but leaves the customer unable to take a specific next action.

Career paths: employer archetypes, title variants, direct-employer/recruiter/association/network channels and current compensation evidence where valid.
Business paths: buyer/problem, offer shape, acquisition channels, validation conversations, explicit economics scenarios, startup/regulatory questions and stop/continue criteria.

Human reviewer checks for generic advice, unsupported precision, repetitive opportunities, hidden deal-breakers and recommendations that ignore lifestyle constraints.
