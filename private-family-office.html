PRIVATE FAMILY OFFICE™

Make the life you built easier to understand.

A household information, continuity and organization framework built around eight intuitive rooms.

THE EIGHT ROOMS™

Know what you have. Know where it is. Know what matters next.

Private Family Office™ is designed to make a complex household easier for you—and trusted people—to navigate when necessary.

01

My Life

Blueprint, priorities, goals, experiments, key dates and major decisions.

02

My Family

Household, children, grandchildren, trusted contacts, responsibilities and traditions.

03

My Financial World

An organized inventory of institutions, income sources, insurance, liabilities and professional contacts.

04

My Property

Homes, vehicles, valuables, vendors, maintenance information and instructions.

05

My Documents

An index of estate, insurance, property, identity, business and other important records.

06

My Experiences

Travel ideas, learning goals, family experiences, completed adventures and memories.

07

My Continuity

Emergency contacts, incapacity preparation, surviving-spouse information and action checklists.

08

My Legacy

Stories, values, family history, messages, traditions, recipes and photographs.

WHY IT MATTERS

Organization becomes most valuable when someone else suddenly needs it.

A household may be financially successful and still be difficult for a spouse, child or trusted person to navigate. Private Family Office™ is intended to reduce that friction while keeping permissions controlled and information purposeful.
