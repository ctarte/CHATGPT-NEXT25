# NEXT25 Visual Completion Plan
Production work must continue visual development.

## Palette expansion
Keep deep navy, ivory and brushed gold anchors. Add restrained muted sage (renewal/learning), slate blue (intelligence/trust), warm clay/rust (creativity/action), and soft sand (editorial backgrounds).

## Photography library
Final target approximately 8–12 strong editorial assets across the full commercial site, not an image in every section:
horizon/exploration; purposeful second-career work; teaching/mentoring; creative making/writing; thoughtful small-business ownership; learning/research; travel/experience; family/intergenerational legacy; community/contribution.

Adults roughly 50–75 should look capable, contemporary and varied. Prefer natural environments, real workspaces, warm directional light, candid/editorial composition and room for text.
Avoid golf/beach-chair retirement clichés, staged handshakes, fake boardrooms, excessive luxury signals and generic stock smiles.

Every production phase after Phase 19 includes visual QA: color balance, image quality, cropping, responsive behavior, contrast, page rhythm and whether imagery advances the story.
